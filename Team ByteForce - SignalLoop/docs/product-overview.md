# SignalLoop — Product Overview

## Summary

SignalLoop is an AI-assisted campus incident management platform for tertiary institutions. It helps campus communities report issues and helps responsible teams triage, assign, track, and resolve them.

**Tagline:** From report to resolution.

## Problem

Campus problems can be reported through fragmented channels, making it hard for reporters to know whether an issue was received, who owns it, or when it is resolved. Campus teams may also receive incomplete or duplicate reports and lack a shared view of priorities.

## Product Goal

Create a clear, trackable path from an incident report to a verified resolution.

## Target Users

1. **Students and staff:** submit reports and receive status updates.
2. **Campus administrators:** review reports, prioritize incidents, assign departments, and monitor outcomes.
3. **Maintenance teams:** manage assigned tasks and communicate progress.

## Product Principles

- Mobile-first reporting
- Clear status and ownership
- Human review of AI recommendations
- Accessible, plain-language interactions
- Respect for reporter privacy
- Clear distinction between saved drafts and confirmed submissions

## MVP Scope

- Authentication and role-aware access
- Create and view incident reports
- AI-assisted report structuring, category, summary, priority, and possible duplicate suggestions
- Admin triage and department assignment
- Maintenance work queue and resolution updates
- Notifications and incident status history
- Basic analytics
- Responsive layouts

## Out of Scope Until Explicitly Planned

- Guaranteed emergency response
- Automatic disciplinary decisions
- Fully autonomous incident prioritization or closure
- Claims of offline synchronization or push notifications before implementation and testing

## Success Measures to Validate

- Time required to submit a report
- Percentage of reports with enough information to act
- Time from submission to first review
- Time from assignment to resolution
- Duplicate-report rate
- Reporter satisfaction and accessibility feedback
